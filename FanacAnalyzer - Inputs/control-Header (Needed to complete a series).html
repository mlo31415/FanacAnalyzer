Fanzines Needed to Complete a Series

These are the fanzine series on Fanac.org whose issues are numbered in a way which runs in order, but with issues missing from the run. Each series is followed by the numbers we do not have. A series is taken to have begun at 1, so issues missing ahead of the earliest one we hold are counted along with the gaps inside; issues after the last one we hold are not, there being no way to tell from the page how far the series actually ran. An issue whose number is not a plain number, such as a 12.5, is passed over: it is neither missing nor does it fill a gap.

A series with no whole numbers which is instead numbered by volume and issue is listed the same way, with its missing issues written V1.3 for volume 1, issue 3. There each volume is taken to have begun at its issue 1, and nothing is counted past the last issue of a volume we hold, since how many issues a volume ran to is not something the page tells us.
