Fanzines Needed to Complete a Series

These are the fanzine series on Fanac.org whose issues are numbered in a way which runs in order, but with issues missing from the middle of the run. Each series is followed by the numbers we do not have. Issues before the first one we hold and after the last one are not counted: there is no way to tell from the page how far the series actually ran. An issue whose number is not a plain number, such as a 12.5, is passed over: it is neither missing nor does it fill a gap.

A series with no whole numbers which is instead numbered by volume and issue is listed the same way, with its missing issues written V1.3 for volume 1, issue 3. There the gaps counted are the ones at the start of a volume and inside it, since how many issues a volume ran to is not something the page tells us.
