Fanzine Series with Annoying numbering

These are the fanzine series on Fanac.org whose numbering cannot be read, so that we cannot tell which issues are missing from the run. Either the index page has nothing to read the numbering from -- no Whole column, and no Number column standing in for one -- or it has a column with nothing in it we can read as a number, or the numbers in it repeat or run out of order. Fixing the page here will move the series to the list of fanzines needed to complete a series.
