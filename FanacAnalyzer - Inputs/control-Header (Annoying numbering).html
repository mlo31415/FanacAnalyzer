Fanzine Series with Annoying numbering

These are the fanzine series on Fanac.org whose whole numbers cannot be read, so that we cannot tell which issues are missing from the run. Either the index page has no Whole column, or it has one with nothing in it we can read as a whole number, or the numbers in it repeat or run out of order. Fixing the page here will move the series to the list of fanzines needed to complete a series.
